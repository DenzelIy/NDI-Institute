What a certificate is made of

Thirty credit units over twenty-four weeks, in four blocks. One credit unit is thirty notional learner hours, so a certificate is nine hundred hours of work.

Digital Core6 units
Family Core8 units
Specialist block12 units
Capstone4 units

The shared blocks

Everyone in the Institute takes the Digital Core, whatever they enrolled on: computing environments, professional practice for technical work, and online safety and data protection under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. The Family Core is shared by related programmes, so a graduate of one certificate carries that credit into any other in the same family.

The capstone

A real brief from a partner organisation, a community need or one of our own programmes, delivered to a deadline. You produce the work, a portfolio, and a written account of the decisions you made. You then defend it in person before two assessors. It is the one assessment that cannot be delegated, bought or generated, which is why we can teach AI tools openly everywhere else.

How you will study

Blended, not remote

Forty per cent of each course is on Loop, our own learning platform, in your own time. Forty per cent is live teaching. Twenty per cent is on-site practical work, rising to at least half for laboratory programmes. No practical course is delivered wholly online.

Taught in real workshops

Robotics and hardware are taught in RAIL, the Robotics and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory at Okrika Grammar School, in service since March 2026 and the first facility of its kind in Rivers State. Equipment for laboratory programmes is confirmed in place before a programme is advertised.

Study around work

Choose full-time blended study, evening and weekend, a hub cohort outside Port Harcourt, or distance study with residency blocks. You may change mode once per intake, with Registry approval and no penalty.

Who we admit

The entry requirements are deliberately low. The diagnostic assessment, not your school certificate, decides whether you can do the work.

  • AcademicSSCE or NECO attempted, or a portfolio of prior learning assessed as equivalent. Credits are not required at certificate level.
  • AgeMinimum 16. Applicants aged 16 or 17 need written guardian consent and are covered by our safeguarding policy.
  • DiagnosticEveryone sits it. It tests reasoning, numeracy and comprehension rather than prior knowledge, and is written so that someone who has never used a computer can score well.
  • EquipmentYou do not need to own a laptop. Device access is part of our access plan, not an entry barrier.

What our award is, and what it is not

A programme completed here leads to a certificate awarded by Niger Delta Innovate Ltd/Gte. Niger Delta Innovate is in the process of seeking recognition from the National Board for Technical Education. Recognition is not guaranteed and we cannot give a date for it.

Every offer of admission states this in the same terms. If recognition is granted during or after your programme, we will tell you plainly whether and how it applies to your cohort. We would rather you enrolled knowing exactly what you are buying.
