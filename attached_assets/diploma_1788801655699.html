The diploma pathway

Diploma study is where this Institute is going. The certificate you start in January 2027 is built so that every credit you earn carries into it intact.

We are saying this plainly rather than advertising a diploma we cannot yet award. Below is exactly where the pathway stands, what is planned, and how to put your name down so that you hear first.

Where the diploma stands today

Admission is open for Level 1 certificates only. Diploma admission is not open, and we are not taking diploma fees or issuing diploma offers.

Diploma-awarding status in Nigeria sits with the National Board for Technical Education and requires institutional recognition we do not yet hold. We have applied for recognition and we are building the delivery record that any such application is assessed against. Recognition is not guaranteed and no date can be given.

Nothing on this page should be read as an offer of a diploma place or as a claim that any programme is accredited or government-approved.

Why your certificate credit is safe

The Institute's award ladder was designed from the diploma downwards, not the other way round.

One credit unit, one definition

A credit unit is thirty notional learner hours everywhere in the Institute: roughly ten hours of directed teaching and twenty of independent and applied work. A certificate is thirty units. A diploma is one hundred and twenty. The arithmetic does not change between levels.

Written as outcomes first

Every course states what a graduate can do before it states what is taught. That is what any credit-transfer or national framework mapping requires, and it means no course has to be rewritten when diploma study opens.

Evidence kept from day one

Registry holds your marks, the moderation trail and the external moderator's report for every intake. Advanced standing into a diploma is granted against records, so those records are kept to the standard a diploma-awarding body is held to.

Register your interest

Put your name down and we will write to you when a diploma opens, with the entry requirements, the fee and the advanced standing your certificate carries. We will not use your details for anything else.

In the meantime, the certificate is the way in, and it is a complete qualification on its own. See the five programmes open for January 2027.
