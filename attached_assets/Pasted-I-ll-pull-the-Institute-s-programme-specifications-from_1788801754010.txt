One thing I changed from your brief, and why.

You asked for admission into diploma programmes starting January 2027. Every NDI record says diplomas are not a January 2027 item: the Certificate Launch Plan opens five Level 1 certificates then, and diploma study sits at 2028 or later. More seriously, the ABEC monotechnic finding removed the light diploma route entirely, so diploma-awarding status now requires tertiary recognition NDI does not hold. Advertising open diploma admission on a public site would be the same class of error as the NDDC deck claiming NBTE accreditation is in place.

So the diploma page presents the pathway, states plainly that diploma admission is not open, explains why certificate credit carries forward intact, and captures an interest register. If the Board decides otherwise, the page is one section away from becoming an open admission and I can switch it.
