Admissions

Applications are open for the January 2027 intake, covering Web Development, Data Analytics, Computer Hardware and Networks, Robotics and Automated Systems, and Graphic Design.

We take two intakes a year, in January and July. The closing date for each intake is published on this page. Apply early: the diagnostic is scheduled fortnightly and places are ranked.

Start an application

Entry requirements

Deliberately low, and we mean it. The certificate exists to bring people in.

  • AcademicSSCE or NECO attempted, or a recognition-of-prior-learning portfolio assessed as equivalent. Credits are not required at certificate level.
  • AgeMinimum 16. Applicants aged 16 or 17 need written guardian consent and are covered by our safeguarding policy.
  • DiagnosticAll applicants sit it. It tests reasoning, numeracy and comprehension rather than prior knowledge, and is designed so that a candidate with no exposure to computing can score well.
  • EquipmentNo applicant is required to own a laptop or phone. Device access is part of our access plan, not an entry barrier.
  • ExceptionsTwo certificates carry a prerequisite: Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, both opening January 2028. Those prerequisites are not waived.

The six steps

Each step carries a service standard. If we miss one, tell us.

  1. Make an enquiry

    By the form below, by email, or on WhatsApp. We respond within two working days.

  2. Apply on Loop

    The full application is completed on Loop, our learning platform. It stays open until the published closing date for the intake.

  3. Eligibility and document screening

    Registry checks your documents and confirms your route in. Five working days.

  4. Sit the diagnostic assessment

    Scheduled fortnightly, online or at a hub near you. There is nothing to revise; it tests how you think, not what you have already been taught.

  5. Offer and acceptance

    An offer follows within five working days of the diagnostic. You then have fourteen days to accept. The offer states in plain terms what the award is and where we stand on recognition.

  6. Register

    You register in registration week, receive your student number and your Loop account, and start with the Digital Core alongside every other student in the Institute.

There is no interview at certificate level, except for applicants coming through recognition of prior learning. An interview adds a fortnight and does not improve selection at this level.

If applications exceed places

Places are ranked on four criteria, after reserved seats are applied.

  • 60%Diagnostic assessment
  • 20%Prior academic or work record
  • 10%Motivation statement
  • 10%Portfolio or demonstrated prior work

Seats reserved before ranking

The Registrar reports achievement against each of these to the Board at the close of every intake. A missed floor goes to the Board, not into a footnote.

  • 50% targetWomen, with a 40 per cent floor
  • 30%Rural and riverine communities
  • 5%Applicants with a disability, with the access plan funded
  • 25%Articulation from clubs and partner schools

How you study, and what it costs

Four modes

  • Full-timeBlended. Suits school leavers and full-time students.
  • Evening and weekendFor learners already in work.
  • Hub cohortFor learners outside Port Harcourt, at an approved learning hub.
  • DistanceWith residency blocks, for learners with no hub in reach.

One mode change per intake, on Registry approval, without penalty.

Fees

Tuition is priced per credit unit within a band, so part-time, transferring and repeating students are charged consistently. There are three bands, set by what a programme actually costs to run.

  • Band 1Studio and classroom: Web Development, Data Analytics, Graphic Design and most design and business programmes.
  • Band 2Laboratory: Systems Administration, Cybersecurity.
  • Band 3Heavy laboratory: Computer Hardware and Networks, Robotics and Automated Systems, Mobile Device and GSM Repairs.

The fee schedule, payment plans and scholarship terms are set by the Board and published here before applications close. Sponsored and scholarship places are available; ask when you enquire.

What your offer will say about recognition

The award is a certificate of Niger Delta Innovate Ltd/Gte. We are in the process of seeking recognition from the National Board for Technical Education. Recognition is not guaranteed and no date can be given. If it is granted during or after your programme, we will state whether and how it applies to your cohort.

Our curriculum is mapped to the National Skills Qualification framework. That mapping is a design decision, not a claim of accreditation.
