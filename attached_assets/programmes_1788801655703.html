Programmes

Fourteen certificates, every one of them thirty credit units over twenty-four weeks, built from the same four blocks. Five open for the January 2027 intake.

Each certificate is a Level 1 award: a complete, employable qualification taken from a standing start. The pass mark is 40 per cent in every course, practical components must be passed on their own, and the award requires at least 75 per cent attendance at live and on-site sessions and a capstone defended in person. Certificates are classified Distinction, Merit or Certificate.

Open for January 2027

Detail below covers what is particular to each award. The Digital Core, the Family Core and the capstone are shared and are described on the home page and in each published specification.

Certificate in Web Development

WEB · Family B, Software and Build · 30 credit units · 24 weeks

What it is for. To produce a graduate who can build, deploy and maintain a working website or web application to a client's brief, and earn from it, whether employed, freelance or building their own product.

Where it leads. Junior front-end or full-stack developer, freelance web developer, web officer in an SME, agency, school or NGO, or the technical half of a founding team.

Entry. Institute standard. No prior coding required.

By the end you can

  1. Build a responsive, accessible web interface from a design or a written brief.
  2. Write JavaScript to make an interface behave: state, events, validation, and data from an API.
  3. Design a simple relational schema and read and write to it from an application.
  4. Build and consume a basic API, and explain the request and response cycle.
  5. Deploy a site to a live host with a domain, and keep it running.
  6. Scope, quote and deliver a small client project, and hand it over with documentation.

Specialist block, 12 credit units

  • WEB 121 · Front-End Development: HTML, CSS and Responsive LayoutSemantic HTML, CSS layout with flexbox and grid, responsive and mobile-first design, accessibility basics, and building from a supplied design without inventing your own.
  • WEB 122 · JavaScript and Interactive InterfacesJavaScript in the browser, the DOM, events, forms and validation, asynchronous requests, consuming a public API, and an introduction to one component framework.
  • WEB 123 · Backend, Databases, APIs and DeploymentA server-side runtime, routing, a relational database and SQL basics, authentication in outline, building a small API, environment configuration, deployment to a host, domains and HTTPS.

Assessment. Continuous 30 per cent, practical build assessments 40 per cent, examination 30 per cent, plus the capstone.

Certificate in Data Analytics

DAN · Family C, Data and Artificial Intelligence · 30 credit units · 24 weeks

What it is for. To produce a graduate who can take a real dataset, clean it, interrogate it, and give a decision-maker an answer they can act on.

Where it leads. Data analyst, business analyst, monitoring and evaluation officer, or reporting officer in government, an NGO, health, energy or financial services; freelance analyst.

Entry. Institute standard. Comfort with numbers helps; the diagnostic tests it and the Family Core builds it.

By the end you can

  1. Acquire, clean and validate a dataset, and document what you changed and why.
  2. Write SQL to answer a question against a relational database.
  3. Build a dashboard a non-technical manager can read without a briefing.
  4. Choose and apply an appropriate descriptive or comparative statistic, and state its limits.
  5. Present a finding in writing and aloud, separating what the data shows from what you infer.
  6. Recognise when a dataset cannot answer the question being asked, and say so.

Specialist block, 12 credit units

  • DAN 121 · SQL and Relational DatabasesRelational structure, filtering, joins, grouping and aggregation, subqueries, and writing readable queries against a realistic multi-table database.
  • DAN 122 · Dashboards and Business IntelligenceConnecting to sources, modelling for reporting, chart selection and honest visual encoding, dashboard layout, filters, and publishing and maintaining a live report.
  • DAN 123 · Statistics for Decision Making and ReportingDistributions and summary statistics, variability and uncertainty, comparison and correlation, common fallacies, sampling in practice, and writing a findings note for a decision-maker.

Assessment. Continuous 30 per cent, practical assignments 40 per cent, examination 30 per cent, plus the capstone. Teaching uses our own library of Nigerian datasets, because generic international data teaches the technique and loses the judgement.

Certificate in Computer Hardware and Networks

CHN · Family A, Hardware and Infrastructure · 30 credit units · 24 weeks

What it is for. To produce a technician who can diagnose and repair a computer, install and troubleshoot a small network, and run a service bench that customers come back to.

Where it leads. IT support technician, hardware technician, network installer, school or office ICT officer, or a self-employed service bench. This is the shortest path to income in the catalogue.

Entry. Institute standard. Manual aptitude matters more than academic record here, and the diagnostic includes a practical section.

By the end you can

  1. Specify, assemble, upgrade and safely dismantle a desktop or laptop computer.
  2. Diagnose a hardware or software fault methodically and document the diagnosis.
  3. Install and recover operating systems, drivers and applications, preserving user data.
  4. Install, terminate and test structured cabling, and configure a small wired and wireless network.
  5. Run preventive maintenance and keep an asset register for an organisation.
  6. Quote a job, manage a customer's expectations, and stand behind a warranty.

Specialist block, 12 credit units

  • CHN 121 · Fault Diagnosis, Repair and Component ReplacementStructured diagnosis, boot and power failures, display and storage faults, data preservation, component-level replacement on desktop and laptop, and knowing when a repair is not economic.
  • CHN 122 · Network Installation, Cabling and WirelessSite survey and planning, cable selection, termination and testing, patch panels and racks, switch and router configuration for a small office, wireless coverage, and documenting an installation.
  • CHN 123 · Service Bench Practice, Maintenance and Asset CareRunning a bench: intake and job tickets, parts sourcing and pricing in the Nigerian market, turnaround and communication, maintenance contracts, asset registers, and warranty and dispute handling.

Assessment. Continuous 20 per cent, practical and bench assessment 50 per cent, examination 30 per cent, plus the capstone.

Certificate in Robotics and Automated Systems

ROB · Family B, Software and Build · 30 credit units · 24 weeks

What it is for. To produce a graduate who can design, build, programme and troubleshoot a working automated system: a robot, a monitoring rig, an irrigation controller, a machine that does something useful.

Where it leads. Robotics and STEM instructor, automation technician, IoT and instrumentation technician, prototyping and maker-space technician, and entry to research and prototyping work at RAIL.

Entry. Institute standard.

By the end you can

  1. Read a schematic, build a circuit, and measure and fault-find it.
  2. Select and wire sensors and actuators appropriate to a task.
  3. Programme a microcontroller to read inputs, make decisions and drive outputs.
  4. Design and fabricate simple mechanical parts, including by 3D printing.
  5. Build an end-to-end automated system to a brief and demonstrate it working.
  6. Diagnose a system that has stopped working and repair it.

Specialist block, 12 credit units

  • ROB 121 · Electronics, Sensors and ActuatorsCircuit fundamentals and measurement, breadboarding, digital and analogue sensors, motors, servos and drivers, power budgeting, and signal conditioning.
  • ROB 122 · Microcontroller Programming and ControlMicrocontroller architecture in practical terms, digital and analogue input and output, timing and interrupts, serial communication, motor control, feedback and simple control loops, and debugging embedded code without a debugger.
  • ROB 123 · Automated Systems Build, IoT and IntegrationSystems thinking for a build, mechanical assembly and 3D printing, wireless connectivity and IoT dashboards, data logging, enclosure and field-readiness, and integrating everything into one machine that survives being carried to a demonstration.

Assessment. Continuous 20 per cent, practical build and laboratory 50 per cent, examination 30 per cent, plus the capstone. Taught in the RAIL robotics bay.

Certificate in Graphic Design

GRD · Family D, Design · 30 credit units · 24 weeks

What it is for. To produce a designer who can take a client brief and deliver finished, production-ready visual work across identity, print and digital, and be paid for it.

Where it leads. Graphic designer in an agency, media house, church, school, NGO or SME; freelance designer; print and branding services as a small business.

Entry. Institute standard. No portfolio is required to start. You leave with one.

The Family D core covers visual design foundations and the design process, including composition, hierarchy, colour and accessible contrast, type, user research, sketching and the critique habit. The twelve-unit specialist block covers identity and brand systems, layout and print production, and digital and social formats. Full course detail is set out in the published programme specification.

Assessment. Predominantly studio: continuous assessment, a portfolio of production-ready work, and the capstone.

Moving between programmes

Because the Digital Core and the Family Cores are shared, a graduate of one certificate carries that credit into any other certificate in the same family. Only the specialist block and a new capstone remain. A Data Analytics graduate moving to Data Science, or a hardware graduate moving to systems administration or cybersecurity, is taking twelve units and a project, not another full award.

Certificate credit is also designed to carry forward intact into diploma study when the Institute introduces it, so nothing you complete now is repeated later.
