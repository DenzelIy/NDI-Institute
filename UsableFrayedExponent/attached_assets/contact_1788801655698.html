Where we are

The Institute is based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State. Practical teaching in robotics and hardware takes place at RAIL, the Robotics and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory at Okrika Grammar School, Okrika.

Learners outside Port Harcourt study through an approved learning hub or by distance with residency blocks. If there is no hub near you, tell us where you are; hub locations follow demand.
